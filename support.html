NATA SUPPORT

Support churches serving and planting internationally.

NATA support is designed to help local churches, church planters, and ministry workers receive practical assistance as they serve among the nations.

David Waltz · Co-Founder of NATA International Church Network · PMP, CSM at Compassion International

MINISTRY GRANTS

Request financial support.

NATA may provide grants with the support of other local churches. If support is received, recipients are encouraged, as they are able, to return financial support so that other churches and ministries can be helped in turn.

START-UP SUPPORT

Request help getting started.

For new church plants and ministry initiatives, you can request support in one or more areas.

GIVE TO NATA

Churches can help strengthen the network.

Churches and ministry partners can contribute financially to help NATA develop resources, support ministry initiatives, and serve churches internationally.
